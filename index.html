1. Common Abbreviations (အတိုကောက်စာလုံးများ)

2. Similes (ပမာဏ)

ရှင်းလင်းချက်

Simile ဆိုသည်မှာ အရာဝတ္ထုတစ်ခုကို အခြားအရာဝတ္ထုတစ်ခုနှင့် "as ... as" သို့မဟုတ် "like" ကို အသုံးပြု၍ နှိုင်းယှဉ်ဖော်ပြခြင်းဖြစ်သည်။ ဥပမာ - "as agile as a cat" (ကြောင်ကဲ့သို့ သွက်လက်သော)။

Exercise: Fill in the blanks with suitable similes.

1. I want the language in these legal contracts to be as ____ as ____ so that there is no confusion.
Answer: as clear as crystal/daylight
2. I expected Danny's student apartment to be a total mess, but it was as ____ as ____.
Answer: as clean as a new pin / a Dutch oven
3. She was as ____ as ____. She could think of nothing to say.
Answer: as bashful as a schoolgirl
4. These flowers will make a goldsmith cry because they are as ____ as ____.
Answer: as beautiful as the sunset/rainbow
5. I hope Grandpa wears his hearing aid to dinner tonight because he is as ____ as ____.
Answer: as deaf as a beetle
6. The gymnast was as ____ as ____, performing acrobatic stunts on the balance beam.
Answer: as agile as a cat/monkey
7. Being a good student, she is motivated to learn everything. She is as ____ as ____.
Answer: as eager as a bridegroom
8. I had never met her sister but I recognized her immediately because they're as ____ as ____.
Answer: as alike as two peas
9. The exam is approaching. Zaw Zaw is now as ____ as ____.
Answer: as fidgety as an old maid
10. I did have surgery a few months ago, but I'm as ____ as ____ now.
Answer: as fit as a fiddle
11. The students fell asleep while they were listening to him giving a lecture. It was as ____ as ____.
Answer: as dull as ditchwater/lead
12. The girl is as ____ as ____ to learn everything. She is fully motivated.
Answer: as eager as a bridegroom
13. Even though the manager is very strict with his employees, he is as ____ as ____ with his wife.
Answer: as docile as a lamb
14. The two of them seem to have the same faces. They are as ____ as ____.
Answer: as alike as two peas
15. The spinster was as ____ as ____ when she was praised for her beauty.
Answer: as bashful as a schoolgirl

3. Idiomatic Expressions (အီဒီယမ်စကားလုံးများ)

ရှင်းလင်းချက်

Idiom ဆိုသည်မှာ စကားလုံးတစ်လုံးချင်းစီရဲ့ အဓိပ္ပာယ်နဲ့ မတူဘဲ၊ စကားစုတစ်ခုလုံးအနေနဲ့ အဓိပ္ပာယ်တစ်မျိုး ဖော်ပြတဲ့ စကားအသုံးအနှုန်းတွေဖြစ်ပါတယ်။

Exercise: Rewrite the following sentences, replacing the underlined words with idiomatic expressions.

1. He telephoned his friend to confirm the meeting.
Answer: He called up his friend to confirm the meeting.
2. She depended upon her friends for emotional support during tough times.
Answer: She counted on her friends for emotional support during tough times.
3. The news surprised everyone in the office.
Answer: The news took everyone in the office by surprise.
4. They are discussing the new project plan in the meeting.
Answer: They are talking over the new project plan in the meeting.
5. She reared her children with love and dedication.
Answer: She brought up her children with love and dedication.

4. Third Person Singular (တတိယလူ ဧကဝုစ်နာမ်)

ရှင်းလင်းချက်

He, She, It ကဲ့သို့သော တတိယလူ ဧကဝုစ် (Third Person Singular) နာမ်များအတွက် ကြိယာကို ပြောင်းလဲရေးသားရပါသည်။

  • We / You / They → He / She / It
  • Our / Your / Their → His / Her / Its
  • Ourselves / Yourselves / Themselves → Himself / Herself / Itself
  • Plural nouns (eyes, wings) → singular (eye, wing)
  • are → is, do → does, have → has

ဥပမာ - Computers are powerful machines. → A computer is a powerful machine.

Exercise: Rewrite the following sentences in the Third Person Singular.

1. Igloos are circular snow houses used by Eskimos.
Answer: An igloo is a circular snow house used by an Eskimo.
2. Tepees are conical tents that were used in the past by some Native Americans.
Answer: A tepee is a conical tent that was used in the past by a Native American.
3. Leopards are as ferocious as tigers.
Answer: A leopard is as ferocious as a tiger.
4. Do polar bears live longer than brown bears?
Answer: Does a polar bear live longer than a brown bear?
5. These sugar apples are very expensive fruits.
Answer: This sugar apple is a very expensive fruit.

5. Interrogative (မေးခွန်းပြုလုပ်ခြင်း)

ရှင်းလင်းချက်

ဝါကျများကို မေးခွန်းအဖြစ် ပြောင်းလဲရာတွင် အသုံးပြုသော ပုံစံများ။

  • V1 → Do + Sub + V1 ...?
  • V-s/es → Does + Sub + V1 ...?
  • V2 / had → Did + Sub + V1 / have ...?
  • has/have/had + V3 → Has/Have/Had + Sub + V3 ...?
  • am/is/are/was/were + V-ing → Am/Is/Are/Was/Were + Sub + V-ing ...?
  • modal + V1 → Modal + Sub + V1 ...?

Exercise: Rewrite the following sentences in the Interrogative.

1. Ko Ko wept bitterly when he did not win the lottery.
Answer: Did Ko Ko weep bitterly when he did not win the lottery?
2. Moh Moh suddenly withdrew from the competition.
Answer: Did Moh Moh suddenly withdraw from the competition?
3. I can lose weight if I run daily.
Answer: Can you lose weight if you run daily?
4. She has never been to Laos before.
Answer: Has she ever been to Laos before?
5. A small path leads through the woods.
Answer: Does a small path lead through the woods?

6. Wish (ဆန္ဒပြုခြင်း)

ရှင်းလင်းချက်

"Wish" ကို အသုံးပြု၍ လက်ရှိအခြေအနေ သို့မဟုတ် အတိတ်အခြေအနေနှင့် ဆန့်ကျင်ဘက် ဆန္ဒများကို ဖော်ပြရာတွင် အသုံးပြုပါသည်။

  • V1 → V2 (did not V1)
  • is/are + V-ing → were + V-ing
  • has/have + V3 → had + V3
  • V2 → had + V3

ဥပမာ - Nyi Nyi is sorry that he is not a doctor. → Nyi Nyi wishes he were a doctor.

Exercise: Rewrite beginning with I wish, He wishes, She wishes - etc.

1. I am sorry that I don't have her mobile phone number.
Answer: I wish I had her mobile phone number.
2. It's a pity that I didn't know he was coming.
Answer: I wish I had known he was coming.
3. I'm sorry that I said that. She's now upset.
Answer: I wish I hadn't said that. She's now upset.
4. Don't come through the kitchen with your dirty boots on.
Answer: I wish you wouldn't come through the kitchen with your dirty boots on.
5. Weren't they sorry that they didn't take the time to reflect on their actions?
Answer: Didn't they wish they had taken the time to reflect on their actions?

7. Tenses (ကာလ)

ရှင်းလင်းချက်

အင်္ဂလိပ်သဒ္ဒါတွင် အဓိက အသုံးပြုသော ကာလ (Tenses) များ။

  • Present Simple: V1 / V-s/es (always, usually, every day)
  • Past Simple: V2 (yesterday, ago, last)
  • Present Continuous: am/is/are + V-ing (now, at the moment, Look!)
  • Past Continuous: was/were + V-ing (While, When)
  • Present Perfect: has/have + V3 (yet, ever, never, just, already, since, for)
  • Past Perfect: had + V3 (already, just, after)
  • Present Perfect Continuous: has/have been + V-ing (for, since, How long)
  • Future: will + V1 / am/is/are going to + V1 (tomorrow, next)

Exercise: Rewrite using the verbs in brackets in the correct tenses.

1. While I (do) the language course, I (meet) lots of young people from all over the world.
Answer: While I was doing the language course, I met lots of young people from all over the world.
2. If I (pass) my exam successfully, I (start) an apprenticeship next year.
Answer: If I pass my exam successfully, I will start an apprenticeship next year.
3. She (never be) to New Zealand before. She wishes she (have) been born there.
Answer: She has never been to New Zealand before. She wishes she had been born there.
4. As long as I (get) back home, I realized that I (leave) my wallet at the office.
Answer: As soon as I got back home, I realized that I had left my wallet at the office.
5. Our teacher usually (brainstorm) us before he (start) a new lesson.
Answer: Our teacher usually brainstorms us before he starts a new lesson.

8. Have + Object + Past Participle (V3)

ရှင်းလင်းချက်

တစ်စုံတစ်ဦးကို အလုပ်တစ်ခုခု လုပ်ခိုင်းသည့်အခါ (Causative Form) အသုံးပြုသော ပုံစံဖြစ်သည်။

ပုံစံ - Subject + have/has/had + obj + past participle (V3)

ဥပမာ - The teacher has asked the students to learn the poem by heart. → The teacher has had the poem learned by heart.

Exercise: Rewrite using a 'Have + Obj + Past Participle' construction, omitting the words underlined (the doer).

1. My sister is asking her lawyer to vet the contract.
Answer: My sister is having the contract vetted.
2. Tom asked the technician to reinstall the Windows system files.
Answer: Tom had the Windows system files reinstalled.
3. She has asked the dentist to repair her tooth with a crown.
Answer: She has had her tooth repaired with a crown.
4. He asked the reflexologist to massage his feet yesterday.
Answer: He had his feet massaged yesterday.
5. My father will call the plumber to mend the pipes.
Answer: My father will have the pipes mended.

9. Have + Object + Past Participle + by Noun

ရှင်းလင်းချက်

အလုပ်လုပ်သူကို ဖော်ပြလိုသောအခါ နောက်ဆုံးတွင် "by + Noun" ထည့်သွင်းရေးသားရပါသည်။

ပုံစံ - Subject + have/has/had + obj + V3 + by + Noun

ဥပမာ - The teacher has had the poem learned by heart by the students.

Exercise: Rewrite so that the actor is mentioned at the end of the sentence.

1. My sister is asking her colleagues to proofread her dissertation.
Answer: My sister is having her dissertation proofread by her colleagues.
2. My friend asked [the staff] to save our photographs in the gallery.
Answer: My friend had our photographs saved in the gallery by the staff.
3. She has asked the company to increase her salary.
Answer: She has had her salary increased by the company.
4. She asked the man to repair the broken pipes in her bathroom.
Answer: She had the broken pipes in her bathroom repaired by the man.
5. My cousin will ask the typist to send her file to the manager.
Answer: My cousin will have her file sent to the manager by the typist.

10. Modal Auxiliaries (မော်ဒယ် ကြိယာများ)

ရှင်းလင်းချက်

စကားပြောသူ၏ သဘောထား၊ ဖြစ်နိုင်ခြေ၊ တာဝန်၊ ခွင့်ပြုချက် စသည်တို့ကို ဖော်ပြရာတွင် အသုံးပြုသော ကြိယာများ။

  • It is possible that / Perhaps → may
  • is (not) able to / is (not) permitted to → can (not)
  • was (not) able to → could
  • is going to / promise to → will
  • it is advisable for / ought to → should
  • it is necessary for → must

Exercise: Rewrite, replacing the underlined words with the suitable modal auxiliaries.

1. Perhaps the children are hungry now.
Answer: The children may be hungry now.
2. Ohmar is allowed to go to the pool party with her cousins.
Answer: Ohmar can go to the pool party with her cousins.
3. She had the ability to explain things clearly and concisely.
Answer: She could explain things clearly and concisely.
4. He promises to buy his girlfriend a diamond ring for her birthday.
Answer: He will buy his girlfriend a diamond ring for her birthday.
5. It is advisable to carry an umbrella during the rainy season.
Answer: You should carry an umbrella during the rainy season.

11. Deduction (must) (ခန့်မှန်းချက်)

ရှင်းလင်းချက်

အခြေအနေတစ်ခုမှ ခန့်မှန်းချက် (Deduction) ကို "must" ဖြင့် ဖော်ပြခြင်း။

  • Present active: Sub + must + V1
  • Present passive: Sub + must + be + V3
  • Past active: Sub + must + have + V3
  • Past passive: Sub + must + have been + V3

ဥပမာ - Jane walks to work every day. (live) → He must live near his workplace.

Exercise: Write what you deduce from the statements. Use the cue word.

1. The baby is crying a lot. (hungry)
Answer: The baby must be hungry.
2. She didn't answer my call yesterday. (busy)
Answer: She must have been busy.
3. We can see nobody inside. (out)
Answer: They must be out.
4. I can't find my wallet now. (drop)
Answer: I must have dropped it.
5. She is yawning too much. (late)
Answer: She must have been late.
